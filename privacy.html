Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 12th August 2025

PearlAgric values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform through our mobile application or website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal details (name, email, phone number, address, profile picture)
  • Financial information (bank account or mobile money details, only when provided by investors)
  • Usage data (login activity, project interactions, device information)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process investments and payments securely
  • To personalise your experience on the Platform
  • To communicate updates, project information, and support
  • To improve our services and security

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data only with:

  • Service providers (e.g., Supabase for authentication, data hosting)
  • Payment processors for secure transactions
  • Legal authorities if required by law

4. Data Security

We implement strong technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request corrections or updates
  • Request account deletion and data removal
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

6. Cookies & Tracking

PearlAgric may use cookies or similar technologies to enhance user experience, analyse trends, and improve platform performance.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via email or in-app notifications.

8. Contact Us

If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact us at:

  • privacy@pearlagric.com
  • www.pearlagric.com
